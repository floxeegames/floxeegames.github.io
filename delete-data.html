Bus Traffic World: delete your data

Bus Traffic World by Floxee Games has no player accounts and never asks for your name or e-mail. What it keeps, and how to remove it:

1. Stop anything new being sent

In the game open OPTIONS and set SHARE HOW I PLAY to OFF. From that moment no game events or crash reports are sent. Uninstalling the game also stops everything.

2. Ask us to delete what was sent

E-mail floxeegames@gmail.com with the subject Bus Traffic World data deletion. We answer within 30 days. Game events are stored only under a random identifier, without your name, so we may not be able to match every event to you; whatever we cannot match is deleted automatically, as below.

What is deleted, and when

Game events (levels played, rewards, purchases made) in Firebase Analytics: deleted automatically after at most 14 months.

Crash reports in Firebase Crashlytics: deleted automatically after at most 90 days.

Your progress on the phone: removed when you uninstall the game (unless Android backup brings it back). The cloud copy in Google Play Games: delete it yourself in the Google Play Games app, Settings, Delete Play Games account and data, or ask Google.

Advertising data processed by Google AdMob: handled under Google's privacy policy; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

A "Remove ads" purchase stays recorded by Google Play, as every purchase is.

Türkçe

Bus Traffic World'de hesap yoktur, adın ya da e-postan istenmez. Yeni veri gönderimini durdurmak için oyunda SEÇENEKLER menüsünden OYUN VERİSİ PAYLAŞ düğmesini kapat. Gönderilmiş verilerin silinmesi için floxeegames@gmail.com adresine "Bus Traffic World veri silme" konulu e-posta gönder; 30 gün içinde yanıtlarız. Oyun olayları en fazla 14 ay, çökme raporları en fazla 90 gün sonra kendiliğinden silinir. Google Play Games'teki bulut kaydını Play Games uygulamasının ayarlarından silebilirsin. Reklam kimliğini telefonunun ayarlarından sıfırlayabilir veya silebilirsin.
