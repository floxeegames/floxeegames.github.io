Crown Tumble: delete your data

Crown Tumble by Floxee Games has no player accounts and never asks for your name or e-mail. What it keeps, and how to remove it:

1. Stop anything new being sent

In the game open Settings and set Usage data to Off. From that moment no game events or crash reports are sent. Uninstalling the game also stops everything.

2. Ask us to delete what was sent

E-mail floxeegames@gmail.com with the subject Crown Tumble data deletion. We answer within 30 days. Game events are stored only under a random identifier, without your name, so we may not be able to match every event to you; whatever we cannot match is deleted automatically, as below.

What is deleted, and when

  • Game events (levels, boosters, adverts shown) and crash reports: deleted automatically after at most 14 months.
  • Your progress and settings on the phone: deleted when you uninstall the game.
  • Advertising data held by Google AdMob: managed by Google; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.
