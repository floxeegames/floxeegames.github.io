Crown Tumble — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information Crown Tumble (the "game"), made by Floxee Games ("we"), processes, why and how.

1. In short

  • You do not need an account to play. We never ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or location.
  • Your progress, coins, boosters and settings are kept on your device.
  • We count anonymously how the game is played, such as which levels are won or lost and how long they take, to make the game better. You can turn this off in Settings → Usage data; from then on nothing is sent.
  • The game shows adverts through Google AdMob.

2. What we collect

While Usage data in Settings is on (the default), these in-game events are sent:

  • Levels started, won, lost or left: level number, mode (campaign or daily), shots used and left, targets left, stars, best cascade, whether the level was continued, and how many seconds it took.
  • Boosters used (which booster, on which level) and continuations (with coins or an advert).
  • That an advert was shown: rewarded or between levels, and where in the game.
  • The highest level reached.

If the game crashes, a technical error report is sent (Crashlytics).

With these events the analytics provider may record general device information: device model, operating system version, language, approximate country-level location and a random app-specific identifier.

We do not use this information to identify you and do not combine it with other information.

Purpose: To understand which levels are too hard or too easy, where players stop, and whether the game runs properly.

Legal basis: Our legitimate interest in improving the game (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5/2-f). You can object at any time by turning off Usage data in the game's Settings.

3. What stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, coins, boosters, lives, event progress and settings are stored on your device and are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them, unless your phone's Google backup restores them.

4. Service providers

  • Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google LLC): while Usage data is on, process the in-game events above and crash reports. Data may be processed on Google's servers, including in the United States. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): shows rewarded adverts and adverts between levels. To do so it may process device information such as the advertising ID. In the EU, UK and other regions where it is required, Google's consent form is shown first, and Settings has an Ad choices button to change your answer. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not sell your information. For adverts, it is shared only with Google AdMob as described above.

5. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe your child has shared data with us, write to us.

6. Retention

Analytics events are kept in Firebase for at most 14 months and then deleted.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR and KVKK you may ask whether your data is processed, request information about it, ask for correction or deletion, and object to processing. Analytics data is held only under a random identifier, without your name or e-mail, so we may not be able to match a request to a particular player. How to delete your data: https://floxeegames.github.io/crowntumble/delete-data.html

8. Contact

You have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the game.
